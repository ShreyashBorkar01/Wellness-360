Ultimate Do's and Don'ts Guide

Small changes, big results in health.

🏋️ Gym & Fitness

Do's

  • Warm up before exercises
  • Follow proper form to avoid injury
  • Stay hydrated throughout workout
  • Include both strength & cardio exercises
  • Rest properly between sets and workouts
  • Track your progress regularly
  • Wear proper footwear and attire
  • Stretch after workouts to improve flexibility
  • Listen to your body signals
  • Maintain a consistent workout schedule

Don'ts

  • Don't skip warm-up or cool-down
  • Avoid overtraining or lifting too heavy
  • Don't ignore body pain or fatigue
  • Don't compare your progress with others
  • Avoid poor posture during exercises
  • Don't train on an empty stomach
  • Don't neglect rest days
  • Avoid sudden high-intensity exercises
  • Don't ignore hydration needs
  • Don't use improper gym equipment

🍎 Food & Diet

Do's

  • Eat a balanced diet with proteins, carbs, and fats
  • Include fruits and vegetables daily
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Plan meals and snacks ahead
  • Read food labels to avoid excess sugar & salt
  • Prefer whole grains over refined grains
  • Include healthy fats like nuts and olive oil
  • Eat smaller portions frequently
  • Include fiber-rich foods for digestion
  • Enjoy variety to get all essential nutrients

Don'ts

  • Don't skip meals or rely on junk food
  • Avoid excessive sugar, salt, and fried food
  • Don't eat late at night
  • Don't ignore portion control
  • Avoid fad diets without nutrition knowledge
  • Don't overconsume processed foods
  • Avoid excessive caffeine or energy drinks
  • Don't neglect breakfast
  • Don't eat too fast
  • Avoid skipping vegetables and fruits

💊 Health & Lifestyle

Do's

  • Get 7-8 hours of sleep daily
  • Practice meditation or stress relief techniques
  • Go for regular health check-ups
  • Maintain a positive mindset
  • Spend time outdoors & stay active
  • Build healthy social connections
  • Limit screen time before bed
  • Take breaks during work/study
  • Practice good posture daily
  • Follow preventive healthcare routines

Don'ts

  • Don't smoke or consume excessive alcohol
  • Avoid prolonged sedentary lifestyle
  • Don't ignore mental health issues
  • Don't overuse gadgets/screens
  • Avoid unhealthy coping mechanisms
  • Don't skip sleep regularly
  • Don't ignore minor health issues
  • Avoid high-stress situations without coping strategies
  • Don't neglect vaccination schedules
  • Avoid excessive dependency on medication

🧼 Hygiene & Cleanliness

Do's

  • Wash hands frequently with soap
  • Shower daily and maintain personal cleanliness
  • Keep surroundings clean
  • Brush teeth twice daily
  • Use clean clothes and bedding
  • Trim nails regularly
  • Disinfect surfaces periodically
  • Keep hair clean and groomed
  • Use deodorant and maintain body odor
  • Practice safe food handling

Don'ts

  • Don't share personal items like towels or toothbrush
  • Avoid neglecting oral hygiene
  • Don't ignore skin or hair care
  • Avoid littering or unhygienic practices
  • Don't reuse dirty masks or handkerchiefs
  • Don't neglect washing fruits/vegetables
  • Don't wear dirty or sweaty clothes
  • Avoid leaving trash in living spaces
  • Don't ignore bathroom cleanliness
  • Don't touch face with unwashed hands

✅ Quick Practice Checklist

  • Warm up before exercises & stretch after workouts
  • Stay hydrated & follow proper nutrition
  • Maintain sleep routine & stress management
  • Monitor progress & avoid overtraining
  • Eat fruits, vegetables, and balanced meals daily
  • Practice good hygiene & cleanliness
  • Take breaks & limit screen time
  • Regular health check-ups & preventive care
  • Follow posture & ergonomics guidelines
  • Stay consistent with workouts & healthy habits
