Vegetarian Foods

Nutritional Value:

  • Rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and dietary fiber.
  • Good sources of plant proteins (legumes, beans, lentils, nuts, seeds, soy).
  • Contains healthy carbohydrates and phytonutrients.

Health Benefits:

  • Helps in weight management and reduces risk of obesity.
  • Lowers cholesterol and supports heart health.
  • Reduces risk of chronic diseases like type 2 diabetes, hypertension, and certain cancers.
  • Improves digestion and gut health due to high fiber content.

Health Concerns:

  • May lack some essential nutrients like Vitamin B12, iron, zinc, calcium, and omega-3 fatty acids if not well planned.
  • Protein intake may be lower compared to non-vegetarians if variety is missing.

Non-Vegetarian Foods

Nutritional Value:

  • Rich in complete proteins (containing all essential amino acids).
  • Excellent sources of iron, vitamin B12, zinc, and omega-3 fatty acids.
  • Provides healthy fats, especially from fish and lean meats.

Health Benefits:

  • Supports muscle growth, strength, and tissue repair.
  • Improves hemoglobin levels and prevents anemia (due to high bioavailable iron and B12).
  • Omega-3 fatty acids from fish promote brain and heart health.

Health Concerns:

  • Excessive red and processed meat intake is linked to heart disease, obesity, and colorectal cancer.
  • High intake of fried or fatty meats can raise cholesterol and blood pressure.
  • Needs moderation and preference for lean meats, poultry, and fish over processed meats.
