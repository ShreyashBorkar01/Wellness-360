Weight Gain

  • Definition: Increase in body mass, which can be due to muscle growth, fat accumulation, or water retention.
  • Healthy Weight Gain:
    • Happens when calorie intake > calorie expenditure.
    • Focus on nutrient-dense foods like lean proteins, whole grains, healthy fats, fruits, and vegetables.
    • Strength training supports muscle gain instead of fat gain.
  • Risks of Unhealthy Weight Gain:
    • Can lead to obesity, insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart diseases.
    • Excess fat, especially visceral fat, increases the risk of metabolic syndrome.

Weight Loss

  • Definition: Reduction in overall body weight, often through fat loss, sometimes muscle and water loss too.
  • Healthy Weight Loss:
    • Achieved when calorie expenditure > calorie intake.
    • Should be gradual and sustainable (0.5–1 kg per week).
    • Balanced diet with adequate protein prevents muscle loss.
    • Regular exercise (cardio + strength training) improves metabolism and body composition.
  • Risks of Unhealthy Weight Loss:
    • Rapid or extreme dieting can cause nutrient deficiencies, muscle loss, fatigue, weakened immunity, and hormonal imbalance.
    • Eating disorders like anorexia and bulimia are dangerous outcomes of unhealthy weight loss.
